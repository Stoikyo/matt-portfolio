Questions, probably.

Do I need to know exactly what I need?

No. A rough problem, an opportunity or a half-formed idea is enough to start a useful conversation.

Can you work with what we already have?

Yes. That might mean improving an existing product, website or workflow rather than starting from scratch.

Is this a fixed package?

No. The list is a set of starting points, not a menu you have to choose from. The work can be small and focused or cover a full build.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. Small, practical improvements can be more useful than a large transformation project, especially when time and attention are limited.
