### Notes on the about section

**"Build things like Motoru and Supplie" is cut.** It named your two weakest
examples - one closed, one an unfinished exploration. Replaced with the capability
that's actually distinctive: shipping a whole product alone.

**Hedges cut throughout.** "I like making things" → "I make things." "I'm
happiest when" → "I'm at my best when." "I don't have a fixed way of working"
is gone entirely - it was a disclaimer, and adaptability shows better through
the range of work than through a caveat. "Easy-going" went too; it's the kind
of word people use to seem unthreatening.

**Hundi is described as two people doing everything.** "Lead" implied a team
that doesn't exist yet. Naming the size is a strength here - early-stage
founders reading this recognise it immediately, and "I own the design, product
and build" is a clearer claim than a title.

**Motoru and LeasePlease are kept separate.** Motoru ended in a co-founder
split; LeasePlease is an independent build that carries none of its IP. Wording
like "became" or "grew out of" implies continuity of assets and must not be
used - in copy or in any visual device linking the two. They are two entries on
a track record, in sequence, not a lineage.

**Lease of Mind gets its detail back** - content, code, imagery and SEO in under
two weeks. Previously compressed to "a full site build," which hid the scope.

**Hundi is described as co-founding, present tense** - accurate for discovery
stage, no implied shipped product.

**Lease of Mind is unnamed here** ("a novated leasing consultancy") since it's
named in full in the projects section. Change if you'd rather name it twice.
