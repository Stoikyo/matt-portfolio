About - How I work

Make it real.

I make things, mostly digital, always with real people in mind.

I'm at my best when a rough idea becomes something you can click and hand to someone. I turn fuzzy briefs into clear plans, useful interfaces and working first versions.

Most of what I build is my own, and I take on client work directly too. AI runs through all of it: I build with it daily and know where it earns its place and where it gets in the way.

What I do

From rough idea to first version

  • Turn fuzzy briefs into a clear plan and a working first version.
  • Design the flow and interface, then build it far enough in code to feel real.
  • Ship whole products solo: design, build, content and SEO.

With people in the loop

  • Work collaboratively and give straight feedback.
  • Put prototypes in front of actual humans early.
  • Keep what works, change what doesn’t and drop what doesn’t matter.
